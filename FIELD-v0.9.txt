SwirlDesk FIELD v0.9 // Native Control Surface

- SUPER+SPACE and SUPER+SHIFT+S open a real GTK FIELD CONTROL panel when
  Python PyGObject/GTK3 is available.
- No new keybinds were added.
- Panel is edge-anchored and uses the same Coyote/Khaki instrument language as
  FIELD STATUS, AUTH, NTF and PWR.
- Live local state: CPU, memory, thermal, network, WireGuard, notifications,
  audio, battery, display, local/Zulu time and active visual profile.
- Direct controls: system monitor, network, WireGuard, Bluetooth, volume,
  mixer, display, FIELD STATUS, launcher, wallpaper, visual profiles, lock,
  restart UI, theme and power control.
- A Fuzzel fallback is retained for non-FIELD themes and systems without GTK3
  PyGObject.
- No fonts are bundled.
